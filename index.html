GISSV Spring Enrollment Campaign

Campaign URL Builder & GA4 Tracking Taxonomy

This tool adds campaign parameters to GISSV enrollment URLs so traffic from Meta Ads, Google Search, the community newsletter, and school-fair QR banners can be measured in GA4.

Manually generated link

https://www.gissv.org/gissv-home-english?utm_source=newsletter%2C+google%2C+Meta&utm_medium=banners%2C+email&utm_campaign=springenrollment2026

Enter the website URL and campaign information

Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*), and the campaign URL will be generated for you.
